# Advent of Hogwarts 2026: Plan

A plan for the 24 stories of December 2026. Items marked _(suggested)_ are open for change.

## The arc: choosing each other again

**Fifth year is O.W.L. year.** Since September the four friends have been busy and tired. Careers meetings, revision, Quidditch, duelling and relationships have pulled them in different directions. They are still friends. They have just stopped _choosing_ it: they sit at four different tables, and each privately suspects the others have moved on.

December is the month they choose each other again. The mood is **warm and easy**, with a small wobble in the middle that is mended well before the final week.

Three threads run through the month:

1. **The lanterns (light through-line).** On Dec 1, Flitwick assigns the fifth years the **Lantern Walk**, an old O.W.L.-year tradition. Each student builds a lantern that lights from "the thing you carry". The four lanterns are dark or unreliable at first and light properly one by one. They all shine on the solstice walk on Dec 21. Lanterns appear in roughly seven stories and are never over-explained.
2. **Futures.** Each friend has a quiet question to settle, and each settles it by Dec 24:

   | | Question | Answer |
   |---|---|---|
   | Elliot | Potions, duelling or art? | Art, with duelling as a hobby. Their mother comes round. |
   | Finn | Sprout's botanical placement, or the family wand shop? | The placement. His dad is happy for him. |
   | Mira | Fourteen possible careers | Provisionally runic research and teaching |
   | Zara | Captaincy and her sister | A vice-captain to share the load, and a sister who is her own person |

3. **The empty chair.** On Dec 1, Finn notices an empty chair at the table where the four friends aren't sitting. On Dec 21 they add a line to the pact: one chair is always left empty for whoever needs it. On Dec 24 the empty chair is filled.

**The wobble.** On Dec 16, Mira's well-meant solstice schedule makes Zara feel managed, and the others don't turn up. It is a mild misunderstanding, not a fight. By the evening of Dec 17 it is mended through shared work, not speeches. From Dec 18 to Dec 24 there is **no tension at all**, only warmth, comedy and payoffs.

**No frost.** The runic frost isn't due until 2032, so it isn't used.

## Tension check

2025 had almost none, and an overcorrection would make 2026 feel strained. The plan keeps these guard rails:

- **Real friction in only four stories:** Dec 2 (Zara and Amara), Dec 12 (Elliot and their mother), Dec 16 (the schedule) and, mildly, Dec 6 (the loss).
- **Every one is warm underneath** and resolved quickly, mostly within the same story or the next.
- **Nobody says anything cruel.** Misunderstandings come from good intentions meeting bad timing.
- **No tension from Dec 18 onwards.** The last week is for payoffs.
- **Dialogue disagrees only where the story needs it.** Mostly, friends tease, help and joke.

## The stories

Each entry gives the premise, the beats in order, what it sets up or pays off, and the final image.

### Dec 1 (Tue) · Finn · Light: The Lantern Workshop

**Premise.** Professor Flitwick announces the Lantern Walk: an old O.W.L.-year tradition where each fifth year builds a lantern and carries it on the solstice walk on Dec 21. A lantern lights only from "the thing you carry into the dark".

**Beats.**
1. Breakfast. Finn arrives in the Great Hall and sees his three friends at three different house tables, each with their own year-group, revision notes in hand. He sits with Oliver at Hufflepuff. There is an empty chair at the Hufflepuff table beside him.
2. Charms. Flitwick, standing on a stack of books, explains the Lantern Walk. Thorne hands out brass frames, glass panes and wicks. Students try to light them with wand-fire and fail.
3. Finn's lantern lights on its own, a warm gold, the moment he picks it up. He is startled and hides it under his robe. Mira's lantern stays dark however carefully she charms it. Zara's lights in splutters. Elliot's lights faintly, and goes out when anyone looks.
4. The friends compare notes in the corridor. It is friendly but brief. Everyone is rushing to the next lesson. Nobody suggests meeting up.
5. Finn, walking alone, notices that the "something" in his lantern is the worry he's been carrying: that the group is drifting.

**Sets up.** The lantern thread, the empty chair, the drift, Oliver, and the idea that Finn is the worrier.

**Ending.** Finn carrying the glowing lantern up the dark stair, past the Great Hall's open doors and the empty chair.

### Dec 2 (Wed) · Zara · Light with a little friction: Amara and the Knarl

**Premise.** Three months into her first term, Amara has hidden a Knarl (a hedgehog-like creature, suspicious of humans) in the Hufflepuff first-year dormitory. Zara finds out and goes to "handle it".

**Beats.**
1. Zara gets a note from Professor Sprout: Amara has a creature in her dorm and isn't answering questions. Zara jogs across the castle, rehearsing a firm big-sister speech.
2. In the dormitory she finds Amara and the Knarl (called **Nettle**) on a nest of jumpers. Nettle bristles at Zara. Amara says Nettle likes her because she doesn't hover.
3. Zara tries to help, tidying and giving orders, which makes Nettle's quills flare. Amara, patiently, says "I'm fine, Zara. I'm not your project." Zara says the wrong thing: "I'm just trying to look after you." They both go quiet.
4. Hagrid arrives with Sprout. He is delighted with Nettle and agrees to house him in a shed behind his hut. Amara can visit every day. Amara beams. Zara feels useful for nothing.
5. Zara says "good job" gruffly. Amara hugs her briefly and runs off.

**Sets up.** Zara's protectiveness, Amara as a character, Hagrid's shed, and the thaw that follows on Dec 9, 13 and 19.

**Ending.** Zara leaving a tin of chin chin hanging from the dormitory door handle, with no note.

### Dec 3 (Thu) · Elliot · Quiet: A Few Words with Professor Slughorn

**Premise.** Fifth-year careers meetings with Heads of House.

**Beats.**
1. Slughorn's office: crystallised pineapple, armchairs, framed photographs of former pupils. Elliot arrives early and counts the photographs (eleven potioneers, three Ministry heads).
2. Slughorn is warm and enthusiastic. He is certain Elliot will pursue Potions. He mentions he has written to their mother Lucia, and that he'll invite her to guest-lecture next week. Elliot says "I'm keeping my options open" and Slughorn hears "yes".
3. Elliot tries to bring up drawing and can't. They look at the photographs. They mention duelling. Slughorn says it's a lovely hobby.
4. Slughorn invites Elliot to his Christmas party on Dec 11, with a plus-one.
5. Elliot leaves with a pamphlet about Potions apprenticeships. On the walk back they sketch Slughorn's office from memory and, in the margin, draw a tiny door.

**Sets up.** Elliot's unspoken art, Lucia's visit (Dec 7, 12), the Slug Club party (Dec 11).

**Ending.** The pamphlet slid into the sketchbook, closed on top of the drawing.

### Dec 4 (Fri) · Finn · Quiet: The Frostbloom Shift

**Premise.** Frostblooms open only after midnight, one every ten minutes, and only for gentle hands. Sprout needs help picking them for the Christmas Eve feast and takes Finn on a night shift.

**Beats.**
1. Greenhouse Four at midnight. Sprout and Finn, in scarves and fingerless gloves, work along the frostbloom bed. Each bloom opens with a faint chime.
2. They talk while they wait. Sprout says the best Herbologists she knew were those who liked waiting.
3. Sprout offers Finn a six-week summer placement at the **Fenmoor Botanical Gardens**, a wizarding garden in the fens. She has recommended him. It starts in July, the same month his dad usually wants help in the wand shop.
4. Finn is thrilled, then quiet. He says he'll think about it and doesn't mention the shop. Sprout says she won't push.
5. He walks back across the grounds to Hufflepuff carrying a small jar with one frostbloom for Oliver.

**Sets up.** Finn's decision, Oliver's role in Dec 10, and the frostblooms' return on Dec 17 and Dec 24.

**Ending.** Finn setting the jar on Oliver's bedside table and watching the bloom close.

### Dec 5 (Sat) · Mira · Light: Longing Fudge

**Premise.** Honeydukes' new seasonal fudge tastes of whatever you most want.

**Beats.**
1. Hogsmeade, first snow of the weekend. Mira has an itinerary for her date with Priya: tea, bookshop, fudge, lake walk. Each stop has a fallback.
2. Zara and Elliot say they're "doing their own Hogsmeade", and then appear at every stop, always three tables away and badly hidden.
3. At Honeydukes, Mira and Priya try Longing Fudge. Mira's tastes of her grandmother's kitchen (cardamom tea, ghee on toast). Priya's tastes of mint toothpaste: she hasn't slept properly since the Arithmancy challenge started.
4. Mira realises she has been scheduling Priya into a busy day. She cancels the lake walk, they go back to a warm corner of the Three Broomsticks, and Priya falls asleep on her shoulder.
5. Zara and Elliot, caught out, bring butterbeers and apologise. Zara's fudge tasted of wet grass and Elliot's of paper, which they won't elaborate on.

**Sets up.** Mira's habit of scheduling (pays off Dec 16), Priya's real character, and Zara's and Elliot's longings.

**Ending.** Priya asleep, Mira holding the unfinished fudge.

### Dec 6 (Sun) · Zara · Medium: The Loss

**Premise.** Gryffindor v Ravenclaw. Gryffindor lose.

**Beats.**
1. Morning. Tamsin (Chaser) is hit by a Bludger in the first quarter-hour and has to leave. Zara, short a Chaser, keeps Jamie (Seeker) back to defend and score.
2. At 140–0 Gryffindor, the Ravenclaw Seeker catches the Snitch. Ravenclaw win by ten (150–140). Zara, on the broom, sees it happen.
3. The Ravenclaw stands erupt. In the crowd, Mira's blue scarf. She cheers once, sees Zara's face, and stops.
4. In the changing rooms nobody blames Zara, which is worse. Jamie says it was the right call at the time and the Snitch just happened.
5. Zara walks out and finds Mira on the grass with two hot chocolates. Mira apologises for cheering and Zara laughs at last. Amara arrives with a handwritten note: "Cheer up. Nettle says hello."
6. Zara decides she needs a second pair of eyes on the pitch. She'll ask Jamie to be vice-captain.

**Sets up.** The vice-captain, Mira's and Zara's friendship, and the sisters' thaw.

**Ending.** The three of them walking to the castle with the cocoa gone cold.

### Dec 7 (Mon) · Elliot · Quiet: The Parcel from Prague

**Premise.** St Nicholas Day, a day late. The parcel from home arrives on Monday.

**Beats.**
1. Elliot opens the parcel at the Slytherin table. Inside are sweets, a lump of coal (the family joke), and a note from Lucia: Slughorn has invited her to lecture on Saturday and she hopes to see them.
2. Their father has tucked in a pencil drawing of the Prague kitchen. On the back he has written: "Your mother has been looking at the Yule Ball photograph for a week. She hasn't said anything. She hasn't stopped."
3. Elliot reads the note several times, shares the sweets with the whole table, and doesn't eat any.
4. That night Elliot takes the unsent letter out of the trunk (the one about not wanting to be a potioneer). They don't send it. They write a one-line reply to their mother: "Yes, come. —E."
5. The father's drawing goes on the wall beside their bed.

**Sets up.** Lucia's visit, and that their father knows and is on their side.

**Ending.** The one-line reply attached to Elliot's owl, and the owl lifting off into the dark.

### Dec 8 (Tue) · Mira · Light: A Reply in Green Ink

**Premise.** Someone has replied to the friends' note in the library alcove.

**Beats.**
1. Mira goes to the alcove for a quiet hour and finds, under the friends' pinned note, a line in green ink: "Thank you. I come here after lights-out. Is that all right? —W."
2. She investigates, comically over-qualified: handwriting, ink brand (Flourish & Blotts green), the library's late-pass logs. The trail points to first-year Ravenclaws.
3. She finds **Wren Calloway** reading alone at dinner. Wren is Muggle-raised from Whitby and homesick. She writes poetry in the margins of library books and has been using the alcove because it feels like a private conversation.
4. Mira resists lecturing and introduces herself plainly. She says the alcove is for everyone and shows Wren one of the books.
5. Wren asks shyly whether she can ask Mira questions. Mira says yes and sets up a time, then catches herself scheduling.
6. Wren's green-ink reply becomes a running one-line conversation in the alcove.

**Sets up.** Wren, the alcove thread, and Mira's role as a guide.

**Ending.** Mira leaving a book on the armchair, open to a page, with a green-ink line waiting underneath for Wren.

### Dec 9 (Wed) · Zara · Light mystery: The Bauble Thief

**Premise.** Baubles keep vanishing from the Great Hall's Christmas trees overnight. Hagrid blames pixies. It's Biscuit the Niffler.

**Beats.**
1. The trees have lost forty baubles in three nights. McGonagall asks for volunteers to stake out the Hall. Zara volunteers. Amara turns up uninvited.
2. At 1am the two sisters wait under the high table. Amara spots small paw-prints in the dust. She says Niffler.
3. They follow the tracks to the hoard room under the castle (from 2025), where Biscuit lives among buttons and coins. He is delighted by shiny things and not at all sorry.
4. Biscuit won't hand anything back to Zara, who tries to reason with him by force of personality. Amara sits down, takes out a gold button, and trades. Zara has to wait, and learns to.
5. Zara tells Amara she is really good at this, and means it.
6. Biscuit gets one tin star to keep. Hagrid shows up late and approves.

**Sets up.** The sisters' thaw, Amara's skill, and Biscuit's return.

**Ending.** Amara and Zara carrying a bucket of baubles back along the dark corridor, both in step.

### Dec 10 (Thu) · Finn · Light: How to Meet a Wandmaker

**Premise.** Oliver has been invited to the Highlands for Hogmanay and is terrified of meeting Finn's wandmaker dad.

**Beats.**
1. In the greenhouse, Oliver confesses: he has practised shaking hands in the mirror.
2. Finn recruits Zara to play his dad. She wears a false moustache and an enormous tweed coat, and asks Oliver questions about wand cores. It goes wrong in funny ways: Oliver says "dragon heartstring" twice and "unicorn" once.
3. Finn laughs too hard to coach. Oliver, relaxed, says Finn is just as nervous about something else.
4. Finn admits to Oliver (not the others) about Sprout's offer and not telling his dad. Oliver, gently: "He'll want to know."
5. Finn glances at the Remembrall in his pocket and says he'll write. Not yet.
6. Zara, in the coat, delivers a surprisingly good dad speech: "The shop will keep, son."

**Sets up.** Finn's decision (Dec 17, 21), Oliver's confidence, and that Oliver and Finn are a solid couple.

**Ending.** Oliver shaking the hand of a tweed-wearing Zara, and both bowing.

### Dec 11 (Fri) · Mira · Light: The Placement Charm

**Premise.** Slughorn's Christmas party. Each guest's name card slides across the table to whoever Slughorn considers most useful to them.

**Beats.**
1. Mira goes as Priya's plus-one. Priya has been invited as an Arithmancy prodigy. Mira has a "borrowed" velvet robe and no expectations.
2. The name cards keep sliding. Mira ends up beside a retired Ministry runologist. Priya beside a Gringotts curse-breaker. They keep being moved.
3. Elliot is here too. Mira, who knows Lucia has arrived, notices Elliot going quiet. Lucia, glamorous and brisk, greets her son stiffly and warmly at once.
4. Mira can't read it, doesn't pry and does something small: she holds Elliot's untouched glass and keeps Slughorn busy with a lengthy question about runic potions.
5. The runologist turns out to be delightful and invites Mira to write to her anytime. It's the first time Mira has been hunted by a career, not the other way round.
6. Priya and Mira slip out early to eat chips from the kitchens.

**Sets up.** Mira's career curiosity (Dec 23), Elliot's talk (Dec 12), and Priya and Mira's easy relationship.

**Ending.** Mira and Priya sitting on the kitchen steps with chips, Mira's velvet robe covered in grease.

### Dec 12 (Sat) · Elliot · Medium, emotional: Lucia

**Premise.** Lucia Novak guest-lectures the fifth-year Potions class, then walks with Elliot by the lake.

**Beats.**
1. Lucia's lecture is brilliant. She demonstrates a healing draught and speaks with passion about the patients. Elliot sees what they love in it and what they don't.
2. The class applauds. Lucia is genuinely surprised. She finds Elliot afterwards and says, "I thought you might not want a lunch with Slughorn. Walk?"
3. By the lake, awkwardly, in cold air: Lucia says she has seen the Yule Ball photograph. "You looked happy. I would like to know what to call you." Elliot says "Elliot. They." Lucia repeats it clumsily, the way you practise.
4. Elliot admits they don't think they want to be a potioneer. Lucia is quiet. Then she says she wanted to give them every door open, and perhaps she held one shut.
5. Elliot shows her one sketchbook page. Lucia studies it for a long minute, and says, "Show me more, another time."
6. The giant squid surfaces. They both watch it.
7. She leaves on the afternoon train. No grand resolution, only a start.

**Sets up.** Lucia's reply on Dec 24, Elliot's confidence for the banner, and the future with their mother.

**Ending.** The squid's tentacle sinking back into the lake, and neither of them commenting.

### Dec 13 (Sun) · Zara · Light: The Snowball Treaty

**Premise.** A Sunday morning snowball war between first years and fifth years.

**Beats.**
1. Overnight snow. A first-year ambush on the lawn: Amara commands the first years with Wren as her second-in-command. Zara's fifth years are caught off guard.
2. Zara, still stinging from Dec 6, throws herself in with joy, builds a snow wall in minutes and negotiates across it with her sister in the voice of a Quidditch captain.
3. Elliot arrives with hot chocolate, Finn with a tray of mince pies, Mira with a clipboard. They all join in.
4. McGonagall appears at the window of the Great Hall. She stands with her arms folded for a long moment, then demands a treaty.
5. The sisters negotiate: no snowballs in corridors, no ambushes on the way to meals, and one grand "final battle" on Dec 19 (Pyjama Quidditch). The treaty is written on the back of Zara's Quidditch schedule.
6. Wren says she's never been in a snowball fight before. Amara, appalled, throws one at her. Wren grins.

**Sets up.** Amara and Wren's friendship, and Dec 19.

**Ending.** The signed treaty pinned to the corridor wall, with a snowball landing on Zara's head.

### Dec 14 (Mon) · Finn · Light: Tibby's Proposal

**Premise.** The kitchen elves want Christmas Eve off, with the staff and students cooking the feast. Finn is asked to bring it to McGonagall.

**Beats.**
1. In the kitchens, Tibby presents her case with a rolled parchment. The elves have worked every Christmas Eve for centuries, and want one night to eat, sing and rest.
2. Finn is chosen as spokesperson because they trust him. He is not good at asking for things. He practises on Dibby, who is better at the part.
3. In McGonagall's office he stammers through the proposal. McGonagall listens, asks questions and says yes, with conditions: the elves truly rest, and a Hogwarts committee (Hagrid, Sprout, Flitwick, Finn) runs the cooking.
4. Finn runs back to tell Tibby. She cries, then briskly produces the recipe for the Winter Blend hot chocolate "for the cooks".
5. He recruits volunteers: the friends, Oliver, Priya, Amara, Wren.

**Sets up.** The Christmas Eve feast (Dec 24), Finn's confidence, and the elves' wage-reform thread.

**Ending.** Tibby tucking a tin of ginger biscuits into Finn's pocket.

### Dec 15 (Tue) · Elliot · Quiet: The Banner

**Premise.** McGonagall asks Elliot to design the banner for the Lantern Walk.

**Beats.**
1. McGonagall found a sketch left behind on a Transfiguration desk (Elliot's drawing of the lake and the castle) and asks them privately to design the banner.
2. Elliot says no. McGonagall says "Think about it." They draw four versions in the library and tear each one up.
3. Wren, studying in the alcove, finds one of the crumpled drafts in the bin and smooths it out. She says it's the best thing she's seen in the castle. She isn't flattering. She just says it.
4. Elliot's lantern, which has lit only when no one looked, glows in the corridor while Wren is watching.
5. They hang the final banner in the Entrance Hall: a line of lanterns in a long curve over the lake. They sign it "E.N." in small letters in the corner.
6. That evening Elliot misses a pact meeting because they are finishing the banner secretly. It sets up Dec 16.

**Sets up.** Dec 16's absence, Elliot's art in public, and Elliot's lantern.

**Ending.** The banner unfurling over the stairs while students look up.

### Dec 16 (Wed) · Mira · Medium: The Schedule

**Premise.** Mira, anxious about the drift, plans the solstice pact like a project. The others don't show up.

**Beats.**
1. Mira sends the others a schedule by owl: pact meetings at 7pm Wed, Fri and Sun, with snacks assigned. She's pleased.
2. At 7pm she is alone in the classroom. Zara is delayed by a long Quidditch practice, Finn is covered in mud in a greenhouse and Elliot is still painting the banner.
3. Zara arrives at 7.40pm, out of breath. She jokes: "Is this an O.W.L.?" It lands badly. Mira says "Somebody has to organise it." Zara says "I didn't know we needed organising." They both stop.
4. Finn shows up and doesn't know where to sit. Elliot doesn't come at all. Mira packs up. Nobody says anything cruel. Nobody says anything useful either.
5. Mira walks home and passes the Great Hall, sees four lanterns on the shelf in the Charms classroom through the window, and realises she hasn't asked any of the others what _they_ wanted.
6. She rewrites her schedule on a fresh parchment. At the bottom she writes: "Ask."

**Sets up.** The mending on Dec 17, and Mira's habit tying back to Dec 5.

**Ending.** The new schedule with one line on it.

### Dec 17 (Thu) · Finn · Quiet: Four Lamps

**Premise.** The last frostblooms must be harvested tonight, and they only open in a ring of four lamps. Finn needs four people.

**Beats.**
1. Finn has been mulling over how to fix the Dec 16 awkwardness. Sprout's note arrives: the last frostblooms open tonight, and need four lamps at four points.
2. He asks each friend separately. Mira says yes at once, Zara after a pause, Elliot says "Fine" and shows up in paint-spattered robes.
3. Midnight, Greenhouse Four. The four stand at the four corners of the bed, holding lamps, with the plants between them. Nobody says much. Each frostbloom opens, one by one, with a chime.
4. They start to chat about nothing: Zara's loss, Elliot's banner, Mira's runologist. Zara apologises for the "O.W.L." joke. Mira apologises for the schedule. Elliot says they've been hiding the banner. Everyone shrugs.
5. Finn says nothing about the placement, but when the last bloom opens, he tells them about Sprout's offer. They are thrilled, and Zara asks whether he's told his dad. He says "Tonight."
6. Back in his dorm he writes the letter: "Dear Dad, I'd like to take the placement."

**Sets up.** Finn's decision, the group's mending, and Dec 21's letter and pact.

**Ending.** The letter sealed and the owl leaving at dawn.

### Dec 18 (Fri) · Elliot · Light: The Staff Pantomime

**Premise.** The last day of lessons ends with the staff's annual pantomime, "Cinderella (Mostly)".

**Beats.**

### Dec 19 (Sat) · Zara · Light: Pyjama Quidditch

**Ending.** The two sisters in the stands, in matching pyjamas, under the floodlit sky.

### Dec 20 (Sun) · Mira · Light/quiet: Last Night of Lanterns

### Dec 21 (Mon) · Finn · Medium, warm: The Longest Night

### Dec 23 (Wed) · Mira · Light: Portraits' Night

### Dec 24 (Thu) · Elliot · Warm: Christmas Eve, Cooked

**Ending.** The table at candlelight, one chair taken, the sketchbook passing from hand to hand.

## Craft notes for 2026

- **Every story has one concrete magical idea**, introduced in the first few paragraphs.
- **Each friend sounds like themselves.** Reread the voice notes in `writing-style.md` before every story.
- **Warm, not strained.** Characters tease and help each other. Friction appears only where the plan above calls for it.
- **Endings:** mostly an image or a small action. An occasional letter, note or reflective beat is fine, but no journal entry that restates the story's moral.
- **Check each story against the continuity sheet** (to be built) before and after writing.
