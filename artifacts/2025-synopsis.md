# Advent of Hogwarts 2025: Synopsis

A summary of the 24 stories published for December 2025 (`src/data/stories/2025/`), for use as context when writing the 2026 calendar. Facts are recorded as the stories present them. Where the stories contradict each other, both versions are noted; see `2025-learnings.md` for the full list.

## Background

### Setting

- Hogwarts, December 2025, roughly 27 years after the Battle of Hogwarts. The war is history: Harry Potter is mentioned (e.g. his Thestral flight to London), and house-elf rights are slowly improving because of Dobby's legacy.
- The four main characters are **fourth years** in 2025, so they will be **fifth years (O.W.L. year)** in 2026.
- The friends ignore house boundaries, rotating which house table they eat at.
- Their private hideaway is an **unused classroom on the third floor**, furnished over the month with cushions, a relocated armchair, candles and a revived fireplace. One story places it on the fourth floor.
- Mira keeps a journal, *"December Magic: A Personal Journal"*, started on December 1st. Most stories end with an entry in it.

### Main characters

**Mira Patel (she/her)**
- Ravenclaw. British-Indian, Muggle-born, from London.
- Long black hair in a braid, dark brown eyes, reading glasses.
- Loves Ancient Runes (Professor Babbling) and the library.
- Struggles with perfectionism and with needing to prove she belongs as a Muggle-born. Her December arc is learning that "good enough" is enough and that living matters as much as achieving.
- Family:
  - Her parents are scientists: Dad is a chemist and Mum is a GP (Dec 19). Dec 13 instead has Mira working at "her dad's surgery".
  - Her maternal grandmother insisted the family had "something special" in its line. She died before Mira's magic appeared, and left behind a solstice tradition of lighting one candle per loved one.
- Roommate: Claire.
- Romance: at the Yule Ball she dances with **Priya Desai** (Slytherin, Arithmancy). She admits Priya is "very pretty", and her friends urge her to ask Priya out properly after the holidays.

**Finn MacLeod (he/him)**
- Hufflepuff. Scottish, from the Highlands.
- Freckled, reddish-brown hair, stocky, dirt under his nails.
- Gentle, intuitive and emotionally perceptive. Loves Herbology and creatures; volunteers for Professor Sprout and is trusted with the key to Greenhouse Seven. Weak at combat magic.
- Friends with the kitchen house-elves and quietly pays them when they'll accept it.
- Family:
  - Dad runs a wandmaking shop in the Highlands and does Hogmanay first-footing.
  - Mum is a Muggle who makes solstice star-wishes.
  - The family **Remembrall** started at Hogwarts with his great-grandmother in 1947. Finn breaks it on Dec 8; it is mended but now empty of smoke, and his dad gives it to him outright.
- **Gay.** Comes out to Zara (Dec 16) and goes to the Yule Ball with **Oliver** (Hufflepuff, sandy hair, works in the greenhouses).
- Not yet out to his parents, although his dad's letter ("whatever you need to tell me… I'm here") clearly invites it.

**Zara Okonkwo (she/her)**
- Gryffindor. British-Nigerian.
- Athletic, box braids, confident, wears her house scarf.
- Chaser and newly appointed **Quidditch captain**, the youngest in a decade, with a Firebolt. Bold, protective and a natural leader. Learns that leadership means trusting her team, and that vulnerability is strength.
- Family:
  - Dad is British, from Manchester. Mum is Nigerian.
  - Dec 7 calls the family pure-blood, but Dec 9 has her uncle visiting "my mother's non-magical family".
  - Her **Uncle Chidi** (mum's brother) died in a Muggle car crash when Zara was ten; she was in the car. She sees Thestrals.
  - Her maternal grandmother in Lagos makes chin chin and sends kente cloth.
  - Younger sister **Amara** is ten, creature-obsessed, and **starts Hogwarts in September 2026**. Hagrid has promised her creature cards and introductions to gentle creatures.
  - An older sister is implied: she taught Zara to braid hair, and on Dec 12 she told Zara Triwizard Yule Ball stories.
- Went to the ball solo by choice and danced with half the school.
- Considering coaching or sports management.

**Elliot Novak (they/them)**
- Slytherin. Czech, from Prague. Half-blood: mother **Lucia Novak** is an award-winning potioneer (healing draughts); father is a Muggle architect.
- Tall and lanky, light brown undercut, grey eyes, silver rings (one with a Celtic knot). Dry, sarcastic, precise and strategic.
- Abilities and interests:
  - Exceptional at Potions, but **unsure they want to be a potioneer**.
  - Also likes drawing, Transfiguration, wizard chess and curse-breaking.
  - A **gifted dueller**: Flitwick suggests competing, and the older Slytherins mention an inter-school youth dueling tournament in February. Elliot tutors Finn in dueling.
- **Non-binary**: comes out to the friends on Dec 12, and wears a non-binary symbol pendant from Dec 13.
- Keeps a **secret sketchbook** of loving drawings of friends and castle life, discovered on Dec 18.
- Family:
  - Their grandmother (Department of Mysteries researcher, taught them chess, the only one who understood their art) died of dragon pox at St Mungo's two years ago; Elliot sees Thestrals.
  - Their mother doesn't know about their gender: she sent masculine dress robes, which the friends altered.
  - Elliot has **written but not sent** a letter to her about not wanting to be a potioneer.
- Czech traditions: St Nicholas Day, Christmas Eve carp, kolače.

### Staff and adults

- **Professor Flitwick:** still present and active (Remembrall repair, Dueling Club, the ball), although Thorne teaches Charms.
- **Professor Willow Thorne:** enthusiastic Charms teacher, called "Flitwick's successor" and "the new Charms professor". Revives the Dueling Club and runs the Twelve Magical Days hunt.
- **Professor Vance:** stern; teaches Advanced Defense to seventh years and helps run the Dueling Club.
- **Professor Slughorn:** Potions.
- **Professor Sprout:** Herbology; mentors Finn.
- **Professor Sinistra:** Astronomy. Muggle-born; collapsed from overwork as a student, and Dumbledore told her "the stars don't judge us".
- **Professor Babbling:** Ancient Runes.
- **Hagrid:** Care of Magical Creatures. Has a new orange Kneazle kitten; his rock cakes are still inedible.
- **Madam Oakwood:** the librarian. A Hogwarts student in the late 1960s. Her school friends were Margaret Chen (now at the Department of Mysteries), Sarah (died) and Diana (lives in Australia).
- **Margaret Chen:** Ravenclaw; wrote the 1973–74 journal on the runic frost.
- **The Grey Lady (Helena Ravenclaw):** counsels Mira.
- **Peeves:** the poltergeist.
- **Others:** Madam Hooch, Madam Rosmerta, and Mrs Catchpole of Tomes and Scrolls.

### Other students

- **Oliver:** Hufflepuff, Finn's date. Dec 13 calls him a fifth year; Dec 16 says he's in their year.
- **Priya Desai:** Slytherin, Arithmancy, Mira's new friend and possible crush.
- **Claire:** Mira's roommate.
- **Gryffindor Quidditch team:** Marcus and Priya (Chasers), Katie (Keeper), Jamie (Seeker, she).
- **Adrian Greengrass:** Slytherin Quidditch captain.
- **Other names:** Dennis Pritchard; Miranda (Slytherin); a Ravenclaw sixth-year called Aisha.
- **Canon names used anachronistically:** Colin Creevey (photographer), Marcus Flint and Daphne Greengrass (Slytherin fifth years), the Patil twins.

### Creatures and elves

- **Biscuit:** a Niffler with a hidden hoard room in the castle foundations.
- **Kitchen house-elves:**
  - **Dibby.**
  - **Tibby:** the dessert genius, Dobby's cousin, and now paid for special recipes. Her "Winter Blend" hot chocolate has cardamom and chili.
  - **Winky:** a young elf who likes baking. This is not canon's Winky.
- **Greenhouse Seven:** a baby Whomping Willow and a Venomous Tentacula.
- The Thestrals.

### Places and traditions the friends created

- **The library alcove:** in the forgotten old section, full of decades of student marginalia. The friends pinned a note there in December 2025.
- **Biscuit's hoard room:** the friends check it for meaningful objects to return to their owners.
- **The singing armor:** a fourth-floor suit of armor that Peeves now uses for carols.
- **The solstice pact:** every year on the longest night, they light candles, share food and make wishes *for each other*, never for themselves. It is signed in Mira's journal.
- **"The Wonder Seekers":** Finn's name for the group. It is half-joking, and Elliot is "getting t-shirts made".
- **Hogsmeade:** a December Hogsmeade trip as a tradition.
- **The recurring motto:** "Seek wonder in small things."

## Stories

### Dec 1: First Frost
Mira wakes to runic frost on her Ravenclaw dormitory window: Dagaz (awakening), Wunjo (joy), Isa (ice/stillness) and Raido (journey), arranged in a circle. She summons Finn, Zara (in Quidditch gear) and Elliot by owl. Finn's intuition that the message is "layered", like a plant, helps Mira read it as a single concept: pay attention and find wonder in small things.

In the Restricted Section, Madam Oakwood points them to a slim journal by **Margaret Chen, Ravenclaw seventh year, 1973–74**. It records that the frost appears every seven years on December 1st (last in 2018, next in 2032), is a message from the castle tailored to whoever sees it, and that hers said "seek wonder in small things". Zara misses Quidditch practice. That evening Mira starts her December journal, and the first snow falls.

### Dec 2: The Niffler's Hoard
While volunteering in Greenhouse Three before class, Finn finds **Biscuit**, the grounds' notorious Niffler, stuck in a drainage pipe with an over-full pouch. Biscuit stole Zara's gold-nibbed quill last week. The friends free him, with Mira releasing the grate's sticking charm, and Biscuit leads them through hidden passages to a forgotten room in the foundations full of his sorted hoard: buttons, coins, quills, prefect badges, lockets.

Finn takes an old silver locket containing a photo of laughing witches. It belongs to **Madam Oakwood**, who lost it in the library in her sixth year (1968). The photo shows her best friends: Margaret Chen, Sarah (now dead) and Diana (in Australia). She weeps with gratitude.

The friends keep the hoard mostly secret and plan to check it for meaningful items. Finn leaves Biscuit polished Knuts and reflects on heirlooms, including his family's Remembrall. (This story implies he already has it; Dec 8 has his dad send it.)

### Dec 3: Quidditch in the Snow
Zara's first match as **Gryffindor captain** is against Slytherin, captained by Adrian Greengrass. Her detailed strategies collapse when magical snow cuts visibility, and Slytherin goes up 70–30 by playing simply. From the Slytherin stands, Elliot taps their temple — *think, look around*. Zara realizes her team is waiting for plays that can't work, tells her Chasers (Marcus and Priya) to play "playground Quidditch", and trusts them to react.

Gryffindor claws back to 100–90, then Seeker Jamie catches the Snitch, for a 250–90 win. Afterwards Elliot denies giving any tip. That night Zara writes "Things I learned today": plans aren't the point, trust your team, strength can mean letting go, and vulnerability isn't weakness.

### Dec 4: The Potion of Reflection
In Advanced Potions with Slughorn, Elliot's **Veritaserum Cordis**, a potion that only clears when the brewer accepts their own truth, stays murky despite perfect technique. Mira's clears once she lets go of needing an Outstanding.

Alone in the library, Elliot realizes the truth: they're terrified of disappointing their mother, Lucia Novak, and may not want to be a potioneer at all. They don't know what they want; they like drawing, Transfiguration, chess and curse-breaking. Two days later they hold that truth in mind as they add the unicorn hair, and the potion turns perfectly clear.

In the hideaway classroom, Elliot shares this with the group. Finn knows he wants plants but not *how*, and Zara is now considering coaching. They agree that not knowing is okay.

### Dec 5: House Elves and Hot Chocolate
Finn takes the others to the kitchens, via the tickled pear in the fruit painting, for a five-flavor hot chocolate night for the whole school. The elves love Finn and know him by name: **Dibby** greets them, **Tibby** runs the chocolate station, and young **Winky** works with Elliot on spices. Winky says she found baking by trying things, which echoes Elliot's uncertainty.

Tibby reveals that Hogwarts elves can now choose to be paid for special work, a slow change since Dobby, her cousin, died and Harry Potter spoke about him. The elves say what they want: education, respect, and the choice of whom to serve. The friends resolve to listen, pay when they can and support change carefully, not impose a new S.P.E.W.

The service appears in the Great Hall by elf magic. Tibby sends Mira a tin of the Winter Blend with the recipe, signed "For Mira who listens".

### Dec 6: The Dueling Club
Professor Thorne revives the **Dueling Club** with Flitwick and the stern Professor Vance, under rules of respect, control and precision. Zara drags all four friends to sign up. Mira is surprised to find shield charms click for her; Finn struggles.

Elliot turns out to be a natural: dueling is like chess. They disarm Finn twice, then show off an advanced shield-into-disarm combination that wins applause from Flitwick, who suggests competitive dueling, and from older Slytherins, who mention a February inter-school tournament. Finn is visibly crushed.

Walking back, Elliot apologizes for showing off. Finn admits it's hard to struggle while everyone around him succeeds, and Elliot offers to tutor him in defensive casting: honest, no going easy, no humiliation. At breakfast they arrange their first session. The lesson: *how* you win matters.

### Dec 7: Star Charts and Secrets
After midnight on the Astronomy Tower, Mira is redoing a star chart for the fourth time, chasing half a degree that the assignment doesn't require. **Professor Sinistra** conjures chairs and spiced tea and tells her story. As a perfectionist sixth year she collapsed during an exam, and Dumbledore told her, "The stars don't judge us, Aurora."

Mira confesses that, being Muggle-born, she feels she must prove she belongs. Sinistra reveals she is Muggle-born too, and says Mira's worth isn't measured by grades. She grades the ink-blotted chart Outstanding on the spot, and sets homework: do something imperfectly on purpose.

Next morning Mira skips her usual Sunday library session for a walk by the lake, a chaotic snowball fight and hot chocolate. Each friend shares their own "not enough": Finn's wandmaking family, Zara's pure-blood expectations, and Elliot's mother.

### Dec 8: The Broken Remembrall
Finn's dad posts him the **family Remembrall**, which began with his great-grandmother at Hogwarts in 1947, as a loan. Showing it in the Hufflepuff common room, Finn drops it. It cracks and the smoke escapes.

The friends research mending charms. Flitwick repairs the glass with Reparo, but the original enchantment is gone; he offers to teach Finn how to re-enchant it, which is sixth-year work. Finn agonizes over his letter home and sends the Remembrall back.

His dad's reply says he isn't angry. The Remembrall has been dropped by every generation before Finn, it is now **Finn's to keep**, and "the best things in life are a little bit broken". He adds: "Whatever comes, whatever you need to tell me… I'm here." Finn reads this as quiet permission to come out someday. He keeps the Remembrall empty for now, as a reminder.

### Dec 9: Care of Magical Creatures: Thestral Edition
Hagrid introduces **Thestrals** at the forest edge. Elliot and Zara can see them; Mira and Finn can't, so the class pairs seers with non-seers to feed and lead the creatures. Zara guides Mira's hand to a shy young female; Elliot guides Finn.

Elliot speaks about their grandmother, a Department of Mysteries researcher, who died of dragon pox at St Mungo's two years ago with Elliot present, and who loved misunderstood creatures. Zara tells them about **Uncle Chidi**, who died in a Muggle car accident while visiting her mother's non-magical family, with ten-year-old Zara in the back seat. She now sees Thestrals as symbols of having survived.

Walking back, Elliot adds that their grandmother was the first person they talked to about "not feeling like just a boy", and that she simply asked what they wanted to be called. (This partly anticipates Dec 12.) Mira journals about trusting what you cannot see.

### Dec 10: The Library After Midnight
Working late on an Ancient Runes essay after Professor Babbling asked for "deeper analysis", Mira explores the library's forgotten old section. Behind a tall shelf she finds an **alcove**: a worn armchair and about twenty personal books (*Alice*, *Pride and Prejudice*, *Magical Theory*, poetry) dense with decades of student **marginalia**. The notes include encouragement before O.W.L.s, a 1989 "he said yes", replies across years, and "your story isn't over".

Mira adds her own note (M.P., 2025). Finn arrives, sleepless and reading about the Tentacula after "yesterday's excitement" — an anachronism, since the Greenhouse Seven incident is on Dec 15. They agree to keep the place special rather than secret.

The next evening all four visit. Zara finds a Quidditch book, Finn a debate about Mimbulus mimbletonia, and Elliot poetry. They pin a jointly written note for future finders: "You're not alone… You're part of it now. —M.P., F.M., Z.O., E.N." Mira understands that depth means connection.

### Dec 11: Transfiguration Troubles
McGonagall sets **teacup-to-turtle** transfiguration: a turtle must be viable, not just look right. All four fail in their own ways. Mira over-theorizes anatomy, Elliot wants a methodology, Zara forces it, and Finn's turtle looks right but scrapes like ceramic.

In their third-floor classroom they combine approaches on the blackboard: Mira's theory, Elliot's flowchart (intention, initiation, sequence), Zara's confidence, and Finn's idea of *asking* the teacup to become a turtle, working with its nature. Elliot succeeds first, then each of the others. Each turtle reflects its maker: Zara's adventurous, Mira's bookish, Finn's contented. They keep practicing and correcting each other, and suspect McGonagall planned it. Lesson: being different, combined, makes them better.

### Dec 12: The Yule Ball Announcement
McGonagall announces the revival of the **Yule Ball** on **December 21st, the winter solstice**, for fourth years and above, with dress robes required. Zara is thrilled; she mentions her sister's stories of the Triwizard Yule Ball. Mira, Finn and Elliot are quietly anxious.

Finn calls a "feelings talk" by candlelight in the third-floor classroom. Each admits pressure about dates and expectations, and Finn hints he isn't sure what he's "allowed to want". Elliot then comes out: they are **non-binary**, their pronouns are they/them (which the friends already use), and they found the words in Muggle library books.

The friends respond simply ("Thank you for telling us"), ask careful questions, and offer to correct people who misgender Elliot. They agree to make the ball theirs: wear what feels right, go with whoever, and leave if overwhelmed. Zara promises Elliot "Elliot clothes". Elliot tells them, "I love you. You're my family."

### Dec 13: Hogsmeade in December
A snowy Hogsmeade Saturday. The friends split up to buy Christmas gifts and meet at the Three Broomsticks at four.

- **Zara** (described wearing a "prefect badge") meets **Hagrid** at the Magical Menagerie. He helps her choose creature identification cards for her sister **Amara**, who is 10 and starts Hogwarts next year, and promises to send Amara his doubles and introduce her to creatures. Zara buys a toy Niffler for Hagrid's new Kneazle kitten.
- **Finn** buys forest-green dragon-hide gloves at Gladrags for **Oliver**, described here as a Hufflepuff *fifth year* who has *already said yes* to the ball, and notes that he has *already told Zara he's gay*. (Both contradict Dec 16 and Dec 20.)
- **Mira**, at Tomes and Scrolls with Mrs Catchpole, buys gift books: magical sports across cultures for Zara, an illustrated medicinal plants guide for Finn, and *The Alchemy of Identity* for Elliot. She also buys herself a constellation mythology book with money from working at "her dad's surgery".
- **Elliot** finds the tiny shop **Peculiar Antiquities** down a side street and buys a silver **non-binary symbol pendant**, which they wear hidden under their shirt.

They toast at the Three Broomsticks and make December Hogsmeade a tradition. Next Saturday is the last visit before the holidays.

### Dec 14: The Singing Suit of Armor
Peeves enchants a fourth-floor **suit of armor** to sing personal roasts at passers-by: Elliot's secret sketching, Zara's Quidditch fumbles, Finn's broken Remembrall, and Mira's "studying ice". Flitwick can't remove the enchantment quickly.

Mira suggests *redirecting* rather than fighting. The friends flatter Peeves's lyrical talent and challenge him to write carols that people would actually enjoy, offering him an audience instead of victims. Peeves accepts. The armor sings "Deck the Halls", holly and ivy grow along the walls, and a crowd gathers and sings. McGonagall arrives, then joins in singing, and awards 5 points to each house for creative problem-solving.

The corridor becomes a festive destination. Peeves even composes a song about the four friends ("Ravenclaw's wisdom and Hufflepuff's heart…"). The story makes running references to an unexplained "Great Pudding Incident". Elliot notes that not every problem can be redirected.

### Dec 15: Greenhouse Seven
After dark, Finn gives the friends a tour of **Greenhouse Seven**, using the key Sprout gave him after two months of helping. The tour includes chromatic lilies, chiming palms, singing flowers, Sprout's experimental self-pruning roses (she may let Finn co-author a paper), and a five-year-old **baby Whomping Willow** that curls a branch gently around Finn's wrist.

At the **Venomous Tentacula**, Finn's sleeve snags and his elbow hits a vine. The plant goes into full defensive frenzy and smashes the fence and pots. Zara holds a Protego. Mira remembers from *Flesh-Eating Trees of the World* that the plant is vulnerable to cold. Elliot shatters windows with precise Diffindo, and Zara adds Glacius. Once the plant slows, Finn calms it with bare-handed "empathic contact".

Professor Sprout arrives and praises their crisis management. She repairs everything, but requires an incident report and daylight-only tours in future. Finn realizes his calm, intuitive skills are real strengths.

### Dec 16: Charms and Chances
With the ball five days away, Finn waits at the Quidditch pitch and, on a walk by the frozen lake, **comes out to Zara as gay**. He tells her he has had feelings for **Oliver** (Hufflepuff, "in our year", partnered with him on a Bouncing Bulbs project) and is terrified to ask him. Zara hugs him, thanks him, and promises discreet moral support when he asks. He isn't ready to tell the others yet.

Meanwhile, in Charms, Professor Thorne pairs Mira with **Priya Desai**, a Slytherin who takes Arithmancy "instead of" Runes, for the Gemino duplication charm. Priya notices that Mira copies an *ideal* quill instead of the real one, and Mira notices Priya builds images in sequence rather than holistically. Both improve, and Thorne praises their partnership.

At dinner the friends share imagined Amortentia scents and family foods (chin chin, masala chai, kolače). Mira senses that Finn and Zara now share a secret and accepts it. Two days later, Mira and Priya study runic–arithmantic correlations and discuss house stereotypes, and Priya praises Elliot. They set a weekly study date.

### Dec 17: The Grey Lady's Gift
At 11pm on a "Tuesday", Mira is drowning in essays, with a looming "Arithmancy exam" and dread about the ball. **Helena Ravenclaw** appears and tells her story: she studied obsessively to live up to her mother, neglected friends and joy, and stole the diadem believing it would finally make her feel *enough*. "Later never came."

Her advice: Mira is already worthy; choose both studying and living; go to the ball and be present, because "being present is the work." Helena says helping students like Mira is how she finds peace.

Mira goes to bed and sleeps well. At breakfast she tells the friends she's going to the ball properly. The next evening she time-boxes her Charms essay to two hours, then joins the others in the hideaway (here "fourth floor"), helping Finn with a temperamental Shrivelfig. She glimpses Helena watching approvingly from the doorway. (Mira's thoughts here also reference Finn telling Zara about Oliver, which she shouldn't know yet.)

### Dec 18: Elliot's Sketch
In a quiet library on Thursday, Mira finds **Elliot's sketchbook**, left on her table with a ring caught in its strap. It falls open, and she can't stop looking. Inside are loving, annotated pencil drawings:
- Finn in the greenhouse ("Patience incarnate").
- Zara mid-laugh ("She doesn't know how bright she shines").
- The four of them in their classroom.
- Mira reaching for the frost on Dec 1 ("The moment she chose wonder over worry").
- Others: Sprout, Hagrid with an implied Thestral, Priya, Peeves, Nearly Headless Nick.

Finn sees it too. Then Zara arrives with a stricken Elliot, who fears they'll be thought creepy. Instead the friends are moved. Zara, Finn and Mira each ask for and keep their own portrait, torn out of the book. Elliot admits they've shown no one since their grandmother died, and that their mother expects potions, not art. The friends urge them to take art seriously.

The book ends with an imagined sketch of the four on a balcony at the Yule Ball. They confirm the ball plan: hair at five in the Ravenclaw common room, meet in the Entrance Hall at seven, and one group photo.

### Dec 19: Winter Solstice Traditions
The story is **set on "December 21st, the winter solstice"**, yet says the Yule Ball is "two days" away. The ball is itself on the 21st; this is the biggest date error in 2025. The friends gather in the third-floor classroom, now with a working fireplace, while their housemates attend parties, and share family traditions:
- **Mira:** her grandmother's candles, one per loved one and one for the returning light. She notes Dad is a chemist and Mum is a GP.
- **Zara:**
  - Her mixed traditions: her dad is from Manchester (English Christmas) and her mum is Nigerian.
  - A kente cloth from her grandmother in Lagos.
  - A photo of **Uncle Chidi**, and remembering ancestors through stories. She tells how he taught her to ride a bike.
- **Finn:** Hogmanay and first-footing, done by his dad, plus his Muggle mum's solstice star-wishes as intentions.
- **Elliot:**
  - Czech St Nicholas Day, and the Christmas Eve carp (their father refused the bathtub).
  - Their grandmother's solstice tradition of wishing for someone else. Her last solstice wish was that Elliot would find people who understood them.

They create **their own tradition**: candles, shared food, and selfless wishes on the stars. They sign a pledge in Mira's journal to gather every solstice. On the Astronomy Tower each wishes for one friend: Finn for Elliot, Mira for Finn, Zara for Mira, Elliot for Zara. Later they confess worries: Finn about coming out to his parents, Elliot's unsent letter to their mother, Zara's captaincy, and Mira's progress on perfectionism.

### Dec 20: The Yule Ball Preparation
On Saturday morning, with Zara reading nearby as moral support, Finn asks **Oliver** to the ball in the library. Oliver says he'd been hoping Finn would ask. (This is the third version of this event; see Dec 13 and Dec 16.)

At five, the friends hold a preparation party in the Ravenclaw common room:
- **Elliot:** their mother sent traditionally masculine green-and-silver dress robes, assuming. Mira and Finn alter them with tailoring charms and needlework, softening the shoulders and adding drape, and Zara lends a silver Celtic-knot brooch for the collar. Elliot: "I look like myself."
- **Finn:** his dad's Scottish formal robes.
- **Mira:** deep blue with silver.
- **Zara:** crimson and gold borrowed from a cousin; she's going solo by choice.

Zara does everyone's hair, a skill learned from her older sister: braids woven through Mira's loose hair, an artistic style for Elliot, and Finn tamed. They plan to meet at seven in the Entrance Hall; Elliot will ask **Colin Creevey** to take photos.

### Dec 21: The Yule Ball
The Great Hall has never-melting ice sculptures, vanishing snow, and an aurora borealis ceiling. Colin Creevey photographs the four. Finn joins Oliver. McGonagall opens the ball, and a floating band plays.

Zara dances with partner after partner. Elliot steps onto a side balcony with Mira: people compliment them without trying to categorize them. **Priya** asks Mira to dance, and they dance and talk for about an hour before agreeing to see each other after the holidays. The friends tease Mira, and Oliver adds that Priya is "very pretty". Finn and Oliver slow-dance.

On the main balcony Finn and Oliver hold hands, and the friends promise to help Mira ask Priya out. At midnight the aurora flares into rune-like patterns that echo the Dec 1 frost. The band stops around 2am. Finn calls it the best night of his life. Mira's journal: "this is exactly what happiness feels like." (Mira returns to find "dawn" breaking, which is impossible at about 2am.)

### Dec 22: The Twelve Magical Days
At breakfast Thorne announces a **scavenger hunt**: twelve clues based on the twelve days of Christmas, teams of up to four. It refers to the ball as "Saturday's", "two nights prior". The friends' route:
1. Sir Cadogan's portrait.
2. Thorne's classroom (copper spheres).
3. A rune clue leading to the Owlery.
4. Fwooper feathers in the greenhouses.
5. Beetle eyes in Slughorn's stores.
6. A math riddle, solved with "Mira's Arithmancy".
7. Zara breaks a shield charm.
8. The library alcove.
9. Enchanted dancing whisks in the kitchens.
10. A constellation on the Astronomy Tower.
11. A potions riddle in the dungeons.
12. "Full circle… bring what you've learned": back in the Great Hall.

A team of sixth years answers the last clue wrongly. Mira realizes the answer is simply to arrive *together*. They come **second**, three minutes behind a Ravenclaw fourth-year team, and win enchanted quills that write in whatever color you think of. Mira is surprised that second place doesn't sting.

### Dec 23: The Gift of Magic
In their third-floor classroom the friends exchange gifts, all opening them at once. The gifts:
- **Finn:** pressed-flower bookmarks with meaningful plants. Mira's has dittany, moonflower and Scottish thistle; Zara's has fire orchid and rowan.
- **Zara:** commissioned carved **wand holders** from "a wandmaker", without noting that Finn's dad is one. Mira's has Ansuz and Perthro; Finn's has plant symbols; Zara also got one for herself.
- **Mira:** books. For Elliot, *The Courage to Be: Essays on Identity and Self*, "found in Diagon Alley last summer" (Dec 13 had a different book). For Finn, a magical herbology book.
- **Elliot:** **sketches**:
  - Mira at the frost window and Finn in the greenhouse, both repeating the Dec 18 drawings.
  - A new one of Zara flying in the snowy match.

Each explains what their gift means. They reflect on the month: Finn says they made him brave enough to ask Oliver; Mira nearly skipped the ball; Elliot could only wear their outfit because the friends had their back; and Zara learned she doesn't have to do everything alone.

Finn proposes the name **"the Wonder Seekers"**. Zara objects, and Elliot threatens t-shirts with their faces. They promise they're "not just December friends". Zara has planned the Astronomy Tower for Christmas Eve.

### Dec 24: Christmas Eve at Hogwarts
Most students went home yesterday; the four stay one more day, with trunks packed to leave tomorrow.

Finn's note summons the others to the grounds. Each names their favorite moment of December: Mira the frost, Zara the friends on the ball balcony, Elliot yesterday's gifts, and Finn asking Oliver. Finn starts an epic snowball fight, and Elliot conjures hot chocolate. They have tea and inedible rock cakes with Hagrid, whose tree is decorated with dragon-egg shell.

At the intimate Christmas Eve feast, about thirty students and staff sit at one long table with no house divisions. Oliver is there. The food includes samosas, plantain and goulash, and there is caroling conducted by Flitwick. Thorne is called "the new Charms professor".

On the Astronomy Tower at midnight, Zara asks whether they'll lose this. Mira says no, because they keep choosing it. They hold hands in a circle as Christmas arrives. Elliot announces a "Wonder Seekers meeting next week". Mira's final journal entry: "Whatever comes next—exams and summers and fifth year and everything beyond—we face it together… It was only the beginning."
