## General Style Guidelines for All Images:

- **Art Style**: Storybook illustration style, similar to middle-grade fantasy novel illustrations
- **Tone**: Warm, magical, heartwarming—never dark or threatening
- **Color Palette**: Rich, saturated colors with warm lighting; winter atmosphere throughout
- **Setting Details**: Hogwarts castle architecture—Gothic stone, torches, magical elements
- **Character Consistency**: Keep the four main characters recognizable across all images using the reference guide
- **Age Appropriateness**: Young adult/middle-grade friendly, no scary or intense imagery
- **Magical Elements**: Include subtle magical details—floating candles, spell effects, magical creatures—to enhance the wizarding world feel
